        How we counted

        • A make counts as coming from its home country, not its owner: MINI, Jaguar and Land Rover are British; Volvo and Polestar are Swedish (in “Rest of Europe”, with Skoda, SEAT, Cupra and Dacia); Vauxhall is British and Ford American.
        • Two badges are split by model. MG’s cars since 2011 (the ZS, HS, MG3, MG4 and the rest) are designed and built in China by SAIC, so they count as Chinese; the MGB, Midget, MGF and TF are British. MG also used “ZS” on a Rover-era car from 2001 to 2005, and those few are counted as Chinese too. Smart’s numbered cars (#1, #3, #5) are Chinese; earlier Smarts are German.
        • Makes too small to place, and DVLA’s “Other” groups, are counted as “Other”.
