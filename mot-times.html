      About these times

      • These are tests carried out, not waiting times. A quiet hour suggests a better chance of a slot; it can’t say whether your garage has one.
      • Times are when each test finished, in UK time. Retests count too: each takes a slot.
      • The postcode area is where the test centre is. Opening hours vary, and few centres open on Sundays, so Sundays aren’t shown.
      • Quietest times only count hours when test centres in the area are clearly open.
