      About this finder

      • Not every charger is here. Check before you travel.
      • No live availability. The list is refreshed weekly; it can’t say whether a charger is free or working right now.
      • Charge times are rough. “100 miles in about 11 min” assumes a typical 3.5 miles per kWh at the charger’s top speed. Most cars charge slower than a fast charger’s top speed, and slow down as the battery fills.
      • Distances are as the crow flies, from the middle of your postcode.
      • Privacy. Only the first half of your postcode (like BN1) is used to download that district’s postcodes, and chargers are downloaded by 20 km map square. Your full postcode, or your location if you use it, never leaves this device.
