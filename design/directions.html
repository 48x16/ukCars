House style directions

Three directions for What's on UK roads, using the real components and data. All three keep GOV.UK Frontend's accessible behaviour, the yellow focus state, and black, white and pink.

A. Editorial B. Number plate C. Instrument panel

A. Editorial

  • A magazine long-read: warm paper, a serif display face (Fraunces), Inter for reading.
  • Pink works like a highlighter pen: under key figures and links, never as small text on paper.
  • Best for: the story page. Calm, trustworthy, lots of room for annotations.

B. Number plate

  • Bold and automotive: a wide, heavy grotesque (Archivo Expanded) for headings and figures.
  • Model names as front number-plate chips; pink as a racing stripe.
  • Best for: personality and social sharing. Loud, so it needs restraint on long pages.

C. Instrument panel

  • Swiss and technical: Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono for every figure, a fine grid and small-caps labels.
  • Pink as a single signal light: the one thing to look at.
  • Best for: Explore and data-heavy pages. Precise, dense, very readable numbers.
